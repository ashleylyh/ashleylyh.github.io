Project in the Embedded Systems course focused on designing and implementing an automated pet feeder with feeding control and pet tracking using STM32 boards and IoT sensors.

## Key Technology & Contributions

- Integrated multi-sensor inputs (OV7725 camera, HX711 weight sensor, MPU6050 accelerometer, HC-SR04 ultrasonic) with STM32 to automate food dispensing and detect feeding activity.
- Developed a secondary STM32 board with NEO-6M GPS + LoRa, enabling real-time pet location tracking within a 10 km range.
