Project in the Introduction to Electro-Robot Design course focused on designing and implementing an autonomous car capable of following a white line track and responding to logic-dependent conditions such as turn and obstacles.

## Key Technology & Contributions

- Designed and integrated dual optical sensors and a bumper sensor with Arduino logic, enabling the car to detect track lines and obstacles.
- Optimized motor control through PWM tuning and H-bridge circuitry, balancing speed and precision to successfully navigate curves, 90° turns, and forked paths.
- Implemented real-time decision logic in Arduino C to handle branching paths, obstacle detection, and corrective movements, achieving successful runs during lab testing despite hardware failures.
